## 1. Purpose

Systematic backcasting: if the p-adic (ultrametric) cosmology program is FALSE (the
likely case given the published nulls), what does that imply for the research
program, the framework, and the field? Conversely, if it is TRUE at a level currently
below detection, what would have had to be the case? The exercise maps the decision
space so the next experiments are chosen to discriminate, not to confirm.

## 2. Tiered Forks

### Tier 1 (~20 yr): p-adic structure absent from CMB higher-n statistics (FALSE)
- **Fork 1A (default):** the ultrametric framework's cosmological predictions are
  falsified at the 2-point (A_LPO<3×10⁻³) and higher-n (ε_p<0.096) level. The program
  survives only in domains where ultrametric structure is a *computational* or
  *informational* organizing principle (hierarchical clustering, p-adic QEC code
  classification), NOT a spacetime primitive.
  - **Backcast implication:** the correct 2016–2026 research path would have de-prioritized
    cosmological signatures after the 2-point null and pivoted to computation/error
    correction earlier. The higher-n extension was worth one paper (constraint) but not
    a multi-year program unless a mechanism emerges.
- **Fork 1B (mechanism):** a non-linear amplification mechanism exists in the framework
  and raises ε_p by ≥10³. Then the correct path would have derived the mechanism
  BEFORE the observational test (avoiding the D1-weakening observed in v0.1).

### Tier 2 (~60 yr): ultrametric structure is real but observational channels are wrong
- **Fork 2A:** the signature is in large-scale structure (galaxy bispectrum), not CMB.
  Backcast: 2026–2032 research should have weighted DESI/Euclid bispectra equally with
  CMB-S4.
- **Fork 2B:** the signature is in the trispectrum, not the bispectrum. Backcast: the
  4-point channel deserved a dedicated template and sensitivity study in this paper
  (only the ansatz was given).

### Tier 3 (~120 yr): the discrete/continuous dichotomy is the wrong question
- **Fork 3A:** the correct structure is a *continuous adelic* completion rather than
  a discrete p-adic radix; log-periodic oscillations are then only an approximation.
  Backcast: the field's fixation on discrete radices (p∈{2,3,5,7}) is a map–territory
  error; continuous-adelic templates (no radix lock) should have been the primary
  template family, and the radix-locked family a special case.

### Tier 4 (alternate axioms): ultrametric geometry is not a property of spacetime
- **Fork 4A:** ultrametricity is an *epistemic* structure (how agents represent the
  world) rather than an *ontic* structure. Backcast: the empirical program (CMB, GW,
  LSS) was misdirected from the start; the right tests are cognitive/computational
  (hierarchical representation, p-adic neural architectures), not cosmological.
