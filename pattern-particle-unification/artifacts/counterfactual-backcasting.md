### Tier 3 (~120yr, fork at 2026): spin-statistics as arithmetic shadow
Counterfactual: by ~2146, the spin-statistics relation is taught as the unramified shadow of an arithmetic structure (statistics = phase of a closed-traversal self-mapping; the tree as the state-space). Backcast: requires the tree substrate to survive as the preferred geometry across both regimes — contingent on the whole UMP program, not this paper alone. Recorded as the program-level aspiration; this paper contributes the table, not the program's fate.

### Tier 4 (alternate axioms): the tree is wrong
Counterfactual: if the BT tree is NOT the correct state-space geometry, what survives? The invariant floor = braiding/exchange data (R = e^{2πis}) and the categorical distinction (symmetric vs braided) — both established without trees. The tree is the CEILING (the unifying substrate), not the floor. Under alternate axioms, the unified table's statistics column survives as a translation scheme; the mass/address column falls (already excluded from claims, Stage 4 FRAGILE).
