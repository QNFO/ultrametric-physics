# Core Claim (locked at Phase 0, 2026-08-19)

**WBS:** QNFO.UMP.013 · **Lock version:** v0.1-phase0 · **Status:** LOCKED (Phase 1 due diligence may refine wording, NOT substance, without a new lock)

## The claim

The Standard Model's elementary particles (fermions and bosons) and condensed matter's emergent excitations (quasiparticles and anyons) are one structural object — the **pattern–particle correspondence on the Bruhat–Tits tree** — read in two regimes:

1. **One table.** Every particle-like entity is a labeled pattern of the BT tree: a vertex class, an edge class, or a composite (rational-function) node, carrying a Compton count N_C* that fixes its mass-frequency scale.
2. **Statistics is a tree-automorphism phase.** Bosons = trivial phase (+1); fermions = sign phase (−1, the half-turn of an edge traversal); anyons = roots-of-unity phases on ramified p-adic branches, interpolating the braid statistics between the two.
3. **Regime dictionary.** The Standard Model is the tree at its unramified/archimedean-adjacent evaluation (free particles, exact statistics phases ±1). Condensed matter is the tree with ramification and composition switched on: quasiparticles are rational-function composites (renormalized N_C*), and anyons emerge when the lattice symmetry group quotients the tree by a finite group, opening the root-of-unity phases.
4. **Unification statement.** The SM particle table and the quasiparticle/anyon zoo are two projections of ONE pattern table; the spin-statistics theorem and braid statistics are its two extremal evaluations.

## Falsifiability conditions (pre-registered at lock time)

- **F1:** A fundamental particle with a statistics phase that is NOT a tree-automorphism phase (e.g., a fundamental fermion with integer spin, or statistics outside the root-of-unity set) would falsify claim 2.
- **F2:** A quasiparticle whose N_C* Compton count is NOT a rational function of constituent counts would falsify claim 3.
- **F3:** A physical system exhibiting braid statistics with a phase that is NOT a root of unity would falsify claim 2's anyon clause.
- **F4:** A SM particle that cannot be assigned a unique tree pattern label consistent with its spin, charge sector, and statistics would falsify claim 1.

## What is NOT claimed

- No new dynamics: this paper claims a structural identification, not a new Lagrangian or a new interaction.
- No derivation of the five imported premises.
- No claim that condensed matter can host every SM particle (the regime dictionary restricts which patterns occur where).
- No claim about gravity.

## Novelty bound

The novel content is (a) the unified pattern table spanning BOTH catalogs, (b) the regime dictionary (place/ramification ↔ physical regime), (c) the falsifiability conditions F1–F4. Everything else is imported or already published by QNFO.
