# Universal Ignorance Audit — QNFO.UMP.013 Core Claim

**Target (stated explicitly):** The locked core claim of QNFO.UMP.013 — *Standard-Model particles (fermions, bosons) and condensed-matter excitations (quasiparticles, anyons) are one structural object: the pattern–particle correspondence on the Bruhat–Tits tree, read in two regimes; statistics is a tree-automorphism phase.*

**Instrument:** Universal Ignorance Audit, 15 questions / 5 phases (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21901984).
**Administration:** 2026-08-19, by the research agent, on the Phase-0 locked claim. Answers written before Phase 1 due diligence; not resolved during Phases 1–4.

## Phase 1 — Surface the Structure

**Q1 (Scaffold detection).** Hidden load-bearing assumptions:
(a) *Mathematical realism about the tree* — the BT tree is a map, not the territory; "statistics is a tree-automorphism phase" treats a representation as ontology.
(b) *Structure suffices for unification* — the claim contains no dynamics; it assumes two theories sharing a combinatorial skeleton are thereby "unified."
(c) The ODR premise (Compton count as primitive) is imported wholesale and carries all its unexamined weight.
(d) The regime mapping "SM = unramified evaluation" has never been derived anywhere in the corpus; it is an analogy wearing the clothes of a dictionary.

**Q2 (Map–territory hygiene).** The BT tree is a model of particle states; equating "boson = vertex class" mistakes the coordinate system for the particle. Statistics phases live in Hilbert-space exchange rules, not literally in tree automorphisms. Risk: the "pattern table" becomes a renamed periodic table with the map projected onto nature.

**Q3 (Wobble probe).** Three wobbles:
(i) The elementary/composite line is blurry in BOTH regimes (proton is quasiparticle-like; W/Z are unstable) — the two-regime split may be cleaner in the map than in the territory.
(ii) Anyonic phases come from configuration-space topology in 2D; whether "root of unity on a ramified branch" is the SAME object or a lookalike is unverified.
(iii) Majorana neutrinos and the Higgs (elementary or Cooper-like composite?) stress the vertex/edge classification.

## Phase 2 — Stress-Test the Frame

**Q4 (Inversion).** The opposite — the shared tree structure is a projection artifact: trees are generic, cheap combinatorial objects, and any hierarchy maps onto one. The statistics match (boson +1, fermion −1, anyon root of unity) is a THREE-CASE coincidence, trivially embeddable in any framework. The opposite is not obviously less plausible than the claim.

**Q5 (Falsifiability test).** A world where the claim is false:
(a) A fundamental particle with statistics outside the automorphism set (parastatistics in 3+1D, spin-3/2 boson) — F1.
(b) **A quasiparticle whose effective mass is not a rational function of constituent masses — and the BCS superconducting gap Δ ~ exp(−1/g) is EXACTLY such a non-analytic function. F2 is at immediate risk; this must be examined in Phase 1, not absorbed.**
(c) A world where the Compton cross-ratio / adelic mass-spectrum structure fails external verification — the regime dictionary's empirical anchor.

**Q6 (Invariant extraction).** Under change of place/completion, a single BT tree is frame-dependent; the invariant is the ADELIC object (the product over all places). Finding: the core claim should be stated adelically — the two regimes are two places of one adelic tree, and the regime dictionary is the choice of place. This is a wording refinement candidate, not a substance change.

## Phase 3 — Multiply Perspectives

**Q7 (Radical perspectival shift).**
- A lattice-gauge computational physicist: bosons/fermions = exchange irreps, anyons = braid-group reps, quasiparticles = poles of Green's functions — all established; the tree adds nothing unless it makes a NEW prediction.
- A mathematician: WHICH algebraic group acts on the tree, and where are its characters? "Statistics = automorphism phase" is decorative without a specified group and representation theory.
- A phenomenologist: what collider or tabletop measurement distinguishes this from the textbook story? If none, it is interpretation, not physics.

**Q8 (Externalized ignorance).** Who knows what I don't:
- Condensed-matter theorists know the UMTC classification of anyons (fusion rules, F/R matrices). Blind spot: the claim may only capture ABELIAN anyon phases and miss fusion multiplicities — non-abelian anyons (Ising, Fibonacci) may not be representable as plain roots of unity.
- High-energy theorists know Weinberg's derivation: in 3+1D, statistics ±1 is FORCED by Lorentz invariance + microcausality. Blind spot: the tree phase must explain why it equals the Weinberg-forced phase, or it is redundant.

## Phase 4 — Uncover the Hidden Forces

**Q9 (Power analysis).** Who benefits: the QNFO program — the framing consolidates ODR, RES.009, QP v2.0, and the anyon papers into one narrative. Disclosed, this is fine; hidden, it drives the physics. Silenced: the standard classification literature (Weinberg, Kitaev, Wen) whose priority the novelty claim depends on under-foregrounding. Harmed: practitioners who might adopt a relabeling as physics — opportunity cost.

**Q10 (Protected ignorance probe).** The dangerous questions:
- "Is this unification anything more than a renaming of two well-understood classification schemes joined by a shared picture?"
- "Does the BCS exponential gap already falsify F2?"

**Q11 (Somatic/tacit dimension).** The felt texture: the boson/fermion ±1 columns snap into place effortlessly — real structure. The anyon/quasiparticle columns feel STRAINED — stretched to fit. The ease gradient is itself information: the structure is real where the fit is effortless (±1 statistics), and the map does the work on the ramified branches.

## Phase 5 — Act

**Q12 (Willful ignorance).** I already know: (a) trees are generic and three statistics classes are trivially embeddable, so the burden of proof sits entirely on NEW predictions; (b) Weinberg makes ±1 forced in 3+1D without trees — the tree reading is explanatory only if it predicts something Weinberg doesn't.

**Q13 (Actionable ignorance).** What to do NOW, without resolving:
1. Front-load three kill-tests into Phase 1: (i) BCS gap vs the rational-function premise; (ii) can the tree reproduce abelian-anyon fusion data (e.g., Laughlin ν=1/3 phase 2π/3) beyond the bare phase? (iii) does the Compton cross-ratio empirical anchor survive external verification?
2. Adelic reframing per Q6: regime = place of one adelic tree.
3. Specify the automorphism group and its characters per Q7.
4. If kill-test (i) fails: rescope the quasiparticle premise to "N_C* ratios are algebraic, not necessarily rational," or restrict the claim to weakly-interacting composites.

**Q14 (Relational ignorance).** *(Silence.)* The unknown asks for patience: do not force the table closed this session. The honest posture is to publish the unification as a CONJECTURE with a kill-test ledger, not as an achieved result.

**Q15 (Recursive meta-question).** What question am I not asking? — *"What would make this framework indispensable rather than merely consistent?"* Name one measurement that cannot be made without the tree. If none exists, the paper's value ceiling is pedagogical/consilient, and the abstract must say so. **Seed of the next audit pass:** run the audit on the claim "the tree framework is indispensable to at least one measurement."
