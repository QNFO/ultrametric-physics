## Appendix D: Glossary of Defined Terms

**Absolute value.** A function $|\cdot|: K \to \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ on a field satisfying positive definiteness, multiplicativity, and triangle inequality. [Ch. 1, 2]

**Additive character.** $\chi(x) = e^{2\pi i \{x\}_p}$ from $\mathbb{Q}_p$ to $\mathrm{U}(1)$. [Ch. 6]

**Adele ring $\mathbb{A}_\mathbb{Q}$.** Restricted direct product of all completions of $\mathbb{Q}$; locally compact topological ring. [Ch. 8]

**Adelic quantum mechanics.** QM formulated on $\mathbb{A}_\mathbb{Q}$. [Ch. 8]

**Archimedean.** Satisfying standard triangle inequality $d(x,z) \leq d(x,y) + d(y,z)$. [Ch. 0, 1]

**Bounded Algorithmic Number (BAN).** p-adic representation as (valuation, digits) for exact arithmetic. [Ch. 14]

**Bruhat-Tits tree $T_{N,q}$.** $(N+1)$-regular tree with edge weight $\log q$, geometric realization of p-adic spaces. [Ch. 5]

**Cauchy sequence.** Terms eventually arbitrarily close: $\forall \varepsilon > 0, \exists N, \forall m,n \geq N: d(x_m,x_n) < \varepsilon$. [Ch. 2]

**Complete metric space.** Every Cauchy sequence converges. [Ch. 2]

**Field.** Ring where every non-zero element has a multiplicative inverse. [Ch. 1]

**Group $(G,\star)$.** Set with associative binary operation, identity, and inverses. [Ch. 1]

**Haar measure.** Unique translation-invariant measure on a locally compact group. [Ch. 6]

**Hensel's Lemma.** p-adic Newton's method: lifting roots mod $p$ to $\mathbb{Z}_p$. [Ch. 4, A.4]

**Hierarchy problem.** $10^{34}$ EW-Planck disparity, resolved by tree depth. [Ch. 10]

**Locally constant function.** Function constant on some neighborhood of every point; p-adic analogue of smoothness. [Ch. 6]

**Metric $d$.** Distance function satisfying identity, symmetry, triangle inequality. [Ch. 2]

**Monna map $M_p$.** Projection $\mathbb{Q}_p \to \mathbb{R}$ inverting p-adic expansion; models measurement. [Ch. 6]

**Non-Archimedean.** Satisfying strong (ultrametric) triangle inequality. [Ch. 3]

**Ostrowski's Theorem.** Every non-trivial absolute value on $\mathbb{Q}$ is equivalent to $|\cdot|_\infty$ or some $|\cdot|_p$. [Ch. 4, A.3]

**p-adic absolute value.** $|x|_p = p^{-v_p(x)}$; satisfies ultrametric inequality. [Ch. 4]

**p-adic numbers $\mathbb{Q}_p$.** Completion of $\mathbb{Q}$ w.r.t. $|\cdot|_p$. [Ch. 4]

**Product formula.** $\prod_v \|x\|_v = 1$ for $x \in \mathbb{Q}^\times$; conservation law across completions. [Ch. 8, A.5]

**Scaling ratio $q$.** Positive real $q > 0, q \neq 1$, representing fundamental scale separation. [Ch. 3-5]

**Strong triangle inequality.** $d(x,z) \leq \max\{d(x,y), d(y,z)\}$; defines ultrametricity. [Ch. 3]

**Thermodynamic wall.** Scaling limit of active quantum error correction due to cryogenic cooling constraints. [Ch. 13]

**Ultrametric.** Metric satisfying strong triangle inequality; geometry of hierarchies and trees. [Ch. 3]

**van der Put Neural Network (v-PuNN).** Neural network mirroring tree structure for readout. [Ch. 14]

**Vladimirov operator $D_p^\alpha$.** p-adic pseudodifferential operator (p-adic Laplacian); Fourier multiplier $|\xi|_p^\alpha$. [Ch. 6]

**Wheeler-DeWitt equation.** $\hat{H}\Psi[g] = 0$, fundamental equation of canonical quantum gravity; discretized on trees. [Ch. 12]
