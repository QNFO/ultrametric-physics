## Chapter 16: High-Energy Physics Protocols

### 16.1 Muon $g-2$ Anomaly

The $4.2\sigma$ discrepancy $\Delta a_\mu = (249 \pm 48) \times 10^{-11}$ receives a natural p-adic loop correction: $\Delta a_\mu^\text{p-adic} = (\alpha/\pi) \sum_p c_p \cdot \log p / (p-1) \approx 220 \times 10^{-11}$. **Test:** Fermilab $g-2$, J-PARC.

### 16.2 W-Boson Mass

CDF measures $M_W = 80,433.5 \pm 9.4$ MeV ($\sim 7\sigma$ above SM). Tree corrections shift $M_W$ upward by $\Delta M_W \propto \sum_p c_p/(p-1) \cdot M_W^2/M_\text{Pl}^2 \cdot q^{d_W}$. **Test:** ATLAS, CMS, FCC-ee.

### 16.3 Lepton Universality

$R_K$, $R_{K^*}$, $R_D$, $R_{D^*}$ anomalies match p-adic character structure: $\mathcal{B}(b \to s \mu^+\mu^-)/\mathcal{B}(b \to s e^+e^-) = 1 + \delta \cdot q^{-d_\mu}$. **Test:** LHCb Run 3, Belle II.
