## What Is This?

**Ultrametricity** is a complete, self-contained development of a unified physical theory grounded in ultrametric geometry — the geometry of hierarchies, trees, and discrete scales. It requires **no prior mathematical knowledge**. Every concept, from a set to the adelic field equations, is defined in place. Every operation is explained. Key properties are proved.
