## Sending Protocol

1. **Verify every recipient email** via their institutional/company profile or the paper's contact author — never send to a guessed address.
2. **Send individually** (no BCC blasts, no mail merge) — each letter is personalized.
3. **One follow-up after 7–10 days** if no reply; never more.
4. **Log outcomes** in `artifacts/outreach-log.md` — sent date, response, engagement.
5. **If any recipient responds with corrections:** incorporate into the next newversion (v0.4) and acknowledge publicly — this is the highest-value outcome (external expert engagement).

## Expected value (honest)

- **Realistic best case:** 1–2 substantive replies. A correction or endorsement from any of these authors is worth more than 100 self-published records for an independent researcher's legitimacy.
- **Likely case:** 0–1 replies, but the emails establish your existence in the field's awareness; follow-up work citing theirs gets noticed.
- **Do not expect:** rapid endorsements, co-authorship offers, or immediate citations. The JPCUB framework is [QNFO-internal with zero external citations] — the outreach is the first step toward changing that, and it will take months.
