## Phase 1 — Surface the Structure

**Q1. Scaffold detection.** What hidden assumptions hold this framework up?
(a) That locales have crisp seams — physics decomposes into well-defined
domains of applicability with sharp boundaries. Real validity regimes overlap
and grade into one another; the seam is a model convenience, possibly reified.
(b) That a privileged substrate/image-space split exists (a rendering map
X→V with fixed direction). If observer and world co-render mutually, "the
substrate metric" is not merely undecidable but undefined — C3's premise is
load-bearing. (c) That natural observables decompose into rational counts
(C4 assumes arithmetic finiteness; continuous phase may not). (d) That
"first-person observation" is a coherent restricted channel — a specific
observer/interface/substrate dualism.

**Q2. Map–territory hygiene.** Where might the map be mistaken for the
territory? The locale framework itself is a map; treating "locale" as a
carved joint of nature is a map–territory error of the same kind the paper
diagnoses. OSR ("the territory is relations") is a metaphysical picture, not
an empirical finding — C2 asserts a map as territory. The Monna map is one
candidate rendering; the interface is a model.

**Q3. Wobble probe.** What does not fit? C1 says EVERY physical statement is
a conditional truth — but C2's ontological claim ("the territory is invariant
relational structure") is stated unconditionally. Either the framework's
central claim is itself a conditional truth (then: conditional on what
locale?) or C1's universality is false. This self-exemption is the wobble.
Second wobble: C3 asserts no finite probe can decide the metric AND that
"structure leaks" — the line between probing and deciding is not sharp.

## Phase 2 — Stress-Test the Frame

**Q4. Inversion.** What if the opposite were true? If some laws hold
absolutely, C1 collapses to a catalogue of exceptions and the framework is
harmless but thin; C3 could still survive on its own. If the map WERE the
territory, "burn the map" pedagogy becomes actively harmful. If observers
could decide the substrate metric from inside (a transparent interface), C3
is false and the world is simpler.

**Q5. Falsifiability test.** What world would show this false? (F1) A world
with a law having no domain-of-applicability seam — e.g., a background-
independent quantum gravity in which "locale" cannot even be articulated
would stress C1 at the deepest seam. (F3) A certified finite first-person
protocol — e.g., apparent-size steps at exactly p-adic rationals observed
without outside knowledge of the rendering — falsifies C3. (F4) A confirmed
unit-dependent or place-dependent observable falsifies C4. Each false-world
is concrete; none is currently ruled out.

**Q6. Invariant extraction.** Change the base/origin/frame — what remains?
The conditional-truth catalogue and the interface argument survive a change
of metaphysical base (realism → anti-realism). What does NOT survive is C2's
ontological reading: "territory = relations" is the most frame-dependent
part of the claim. The underdetermination theorem (C3) and the scale-ratio
observation (C4) are the load-bearing invariants; C2 is the dispensable
gloss.

## Phase 3 — Multiply Perspectives

**Q7. Radical perspectival shift.** A radically different observer? A native
of the substrate X (not of the rendering V) experiences the ultrametric
directly; for them the interface problem dissolves. C3 is then an artifact
of observer position, not a property of the world — the paper must own this.
An AI assistant trained on physics textbooks would see "conditional truth" as
a property of textbooks (the map), risking the framework being a sociology
of physics education rather than physics.

**Q8. Externalized ignorance.** Who knows what I do not? Model theorists
know the decidability structure of p-adic vs real fields; they would ask
whether "undecidable from inside" has any relation to mathematical
decidability — possibly a category error to trade on the word. Experimental
phenomenologists would say no measurement protocol for F3 has actually been
designed; "undecidable" is asserted, not demonstrated for non-visual
channels. Critics of OSR (e.g., semi-realism, Chakravartty) would flag
wholesale OSR import as the biggest blind spot.

## Phase 4 — Uncover the Hidden Forces

**Q9. Power analysis.** Who benefits? The framework flatters the synthesist
and licenses program unification; it benefits the QNFO cross-domain program
by making boundaries principled. Who is silenced? Experimentalists (no new
measurement proposed), the naive-realist student (whose map is called wrong),
and the substrate itself (rendered passive). "Burn the map" rhetoric can be
wielded against working pedagogy.

**Q10. Protected ignorance probe.** Most dangerous questions: (a) "Is the
locale framework itself a self-serving map — re-labeling every failed
unification's boundary a 'seam'?" (identity threat to the program).
(b) "If C3 is true, is the ultrametric research program unfalsifiable in
principle from inside?" (threatens the program's core; must be held, not
resolved here).

**Q11. Somatic and tacit dimension.** What does the confusion feel like?
The Q3 wobble feels like vertigo at the exact point where the framework must
exempt itself from C1 — the familiar feel of a map that includes the
mapmaker only by fiat.

## Phase 5 — Act

**Q12. Willful ignorance.** What am I pretending not to know? That
"conditional truth" is largely a restatement of effective-field-theory
folklore; the genuinely novel part is C3's generalization beyond UMP.010's
visual case, and that novelty is thinner than the four-claim structure
suggests. That OSR is contested and costs more argumentative weight than the
current plan budgets. That UMP.010 already carries most of C3's visual
instance.

**Q13. Actionable ignorance.** What can be done with this uncertainty NOW,
without resolving it? (1) Phase 1 due diligence MUST search: prior
"locale"/"conditional truth" frameworks (EFT philosophy, relational
interpretations), metric-decidability literature (mathematical logic),
and OSR criticism — then state novelty honestly against those. (2) Write F3
as a real probe-design question in the P4 register, not a rhetorical
gesture. (3) Keep C2 explicitly labeled as imported metaphysics in the paper.

**Q14. Relational ignorance.** What does the unknown want from me?
[Silence.] The unknown here asks to be held as undecidable — not converted
into a victory. The paper should let the undecidability stand.

**Q15. Recursive meta-question.** What question am I not asking? —
"What would make the interface-underdetermination claim PHYSICS rather than
epistemology: a derivation from a Hamiltonian/measurement model of the
rendering channel, rather than an argument from perception?" This seeds the
next audit pass and is the P4 question the paper must either answer or
explicitly refuse.
