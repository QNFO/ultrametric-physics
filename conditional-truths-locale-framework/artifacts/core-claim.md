## Claims

### C1 — Locale-conditionality
Every physical statement is a conditional truth: it holds only relative to a specified locale (vacuum/symmetry sector, stationary background, inertial frame, medium, energy scale, representation). Canonical cases: "the photon is massless" (vacuum locale; effective masses appear in superconductor/plasma/waveguide locales); "energy is conserved" (stationary background); "spin is angular momentum" (Noether-charge locale only); "c is constant" (local inertial frames). The physics lives at the seams where the locale changes.

### C2 — Map ≠ Territory
The pedagogical map is never the ontological territory. Maps fail quantitatively (spinning-sphere surface velocity > c for the observed magnetic moment; g=1 vs 2.002319…) and structurally (spin is intrinsic, pointlike, quantized). The territory is the invariant relational structure that transfers across locales: the transformation law (SU(2) representation of the rotation group), the commutation algebra [S_i, S_j] = iħ ε_ijk S_k, the gauge-invariant observable (Wilson loop). Ontology: ontic structural realism (Ladyman & Ross) — NAMED IMPORTED INPUT, not a derived conclusion of this paper.

### C3 — Interface Underdetermination (generalization of UMP.010)
An observer constrained to first-person observations inside a rendering interface experiences the metric of the image space V, not the metric of the substrate X. No finite set of first-person observations — apparent sizes, parallax, vanishing-point convergence — decides whether the substrate metric is Archimedean or ultrametric. **The substrate metric is not decidable from inside the interface.** Yet the interface can be studied from inside: what cannot be decided can still be probed — a rendering with structure leaks structure. (UMP.010 proved the visual-perspective case; this claim generalizes to arbitrary first-person observation.)

### C4 — Scale primitives
Stripped of anthropocentric units, the natural observables are counts and ratios: frequency is a recurrence count; α = e²/ℏc is a ratio; temperature is an inverse scale (1/T = ∂S/∂E), not motion. By Ostrowski's theorem the Archimedean real line is ONE completion of Q among many (the p-adic completions for every prime p). Counts and ratios live in Q, which has many places; writing frequency as a real number is a choice of place, not an intrinsic property of the count. The physics is in dimensionless ratios (L/λ_F, λ/λ_T, α), not in meters or seconds.

## Falsification conditions

- **F1 (C1):** exhibit a physical statement that holds across ALL locale changes — a law with no domain-of-applicability seam — or show that a canonical "conditional truth" has conditions that cannot be specified even in principle.
- **F2 (C2):** exhibit an empirically accessible consequence of "substance beyond relations" — an intrinsic (non-relational) state that produces a measurable signature.
- **F3 (C3):** exhibit a finite first-person observation protocol that decides the substrate metric class from inside the interface — a perceptual probe that betrays a non-Archimedean substrate without any outside knowledge of the rendering map.
- **F4 (C4):** exhibit a physical prediction that depends on the choice of units or on the choice of completion (a unit-dependent or place-dependent observable).
