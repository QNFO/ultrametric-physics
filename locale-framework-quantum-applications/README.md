## What this paper is

Quantum computing is usually sold through a small set of recurring claims: continuous-variable
systems are naturally advantaged, more modes mean more power, quantum advantage has been
demonstrated at device level, complex amplitudes are essential, a transmon is a qubit, and
fault tolerance is a matter of years.

This paper treats every one of those claims as a **conditional truth**: true inside an explicit
domain of applicability (a *locale*), false at its boundary. It states the eight most widely
repeated claims, makes each claim's domain explicit, and pairs each boundary with work presented
at the 23rd International Conference on Quantum Physics and Logic (QPL 2026). The boundaries are
not defects — they are where the physics relevant to practitioners lives. The one quantity that
survives every boundary examined is **energy per solution** (joules per solution).

## Why a reader should care

This paper turns every quantum-computing innovation and practical-application claim into a
conditional truth with an explicit locale, then pairs each claim with the exact QPL 2026 paper
that documents where it breaks. The seams catalog T1–T8 replaces a year of vendor roadmaps:
claims are conditional truths, the seams are public, and the energy exchange rate between
paradigms is the currency.

**Premise-depth:** the framework's primitives *locale* and *interface* are imported from the
companion record (UMP.011); OSR is a named input; the energy theorems are imported external
results; the catalog is retrodictive; no dynamical theory of seams is claimed.

## For practitioners

- **§3, Table 1** is a decision tool for a buyer, investor, or engineer: for each of the eight
  recurring claims, it states the locale (where the claim holds), the seam (where it breaks),
  and the QPL 2026 work that documents the break.
- **§4** reads like a spec-sheet: what each paradigm is actually benchmarked to deliver, and
  which roadmap numbers are conditional on unproven assumptions.
- **§5** asks "what does a correct answer cost" and answers in energy per solution.
- **§6** surveys practitioner-oriented work at QPL 2026 and lists deployable items.
