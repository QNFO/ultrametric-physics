## Core claim (locked, P6)

**C₀:** The locale framework transfers to quantum computing. Every innovation and practical-application claim of quantum computing — "advantage", "utility", "fault tolerance by 2030", "CV outperforms DV" — is a conditional truth with an implicit locale. The seams where those claims fail to transfer are where the practitioner-relevant physics lives; the practitioner (engineer, investor, buyer) experiences a rendering (spec sheets, benchmarks, roadmaps), not the substrate; and the natural observables of the practitioner domain are counts and ratios — of which joules-per-solution is the composite scale primitive.

Falsifiable core (inherited from UMP.011 C3, instantiated): the substrate metric of the practitioner interface is not decidable from inside that interface; what cannot be decided can still be probed — a rendering with structure leaks structure. The seams catalog T1–T8 is the probe set: each entry pairs a claim with the QPL 2026 document that publicly documents where it breaks.
