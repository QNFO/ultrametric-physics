## The four moves, expanded

**Move 1 (Archimedean anatomy).** Apparent size ~ h/d presupposes (a) similar triangles — ratio + angle both Archimedean notions, (b) additive geodesic distance — the Archimedean property itself, (c) projective closure over R — vanishing points are ideal points of a complete ordered field. Two-point perspective is not an approximation of the eye; it is the projection of a Euclidean scene, and its fidelity is evidence about the projection, not the scene.

**Move 2 (Ultrametric demolition).** d(x,z) ≤ max(d(x,y), d(y,z)) implies: all triangles isosceles; balls clopen and boundaryless (every point is a center); the space totally disconnected; no nonconstant continuous f: [0,1] → X. Hence no rays, no angles, no smooth scaling; imposed size laws jump by factors of p. Direct ultrametric perception is falsified by smooth vision — recorded as C3/C4, not avoided.

**Move 3 (Rendering lemma).** Perception = surjection R: X → V; measurements live in V only. The Monna map M: Q_p → R (surjective, necessarily non-continuous) is the explicit candidate; Pitkänen's canonical identification is the same construction (hep-th/9410058, hep-th/9506097). Corpus anchors: M11 (19438889), UNO §1.1 (19040000).

**Move 4 (Underdetermination).** Both substrates render into the same V; observation records are identical; the metric is undecidable from inside. The equivalence is the finding. Ontological consequences: continuum demoted to appearance; distance = computational isolation (UNO); identity tree-based; Ostrowski makes the Archimedean choice a postulate validated only by interaction.
